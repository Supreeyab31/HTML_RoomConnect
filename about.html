About RoomConnect

Why RoomConnect exists

Finding the right roommate in Ingolstadt should feel safe, informed, and compatible — not random. RoomConnect was created for KU and THI students in Ingolstadt who are looking for a more trustworthy and structured way to find shared housing. It supports not only new students arriving in the city, but also those who already live, need a new flatmate after someone moves out, or want to change their current housing situation. At the same time, it also creates value for landlords by helping them connect with more reliable, better-matched, and potentially longer-term student tenants.

The challenge

Student housing decisions are often made quickly and with limited information. New students may not know which housing is trustworthy, while existing members may struggle to find a compatible new roommate when a room becomes available. Landlords face a similar problem: they often have to choose tenants with very little insight into compatibility, reliability, or whether the shared living situation is likely to remain stable.

In both cases, a poor match can lead to stress, discomfort, and avoidable conflict. Shared housing is not only about finding a free room — it is about finding the right people and the right living situation.

Our approach

RoomConnect takes a more structured and student-centered approach to shared housing. Instead of relying only on first impressions or random messages, the platform combines verified student access, lifestyle preferences, and compatibility-based matching.

The goal is to support better better housing decisions not only for students searching for a housing and existing members searching for the right new roommate, but also for landlords who want more stable, trustworthy, and better-matched tenants.

Verified

RoomConnect is designed as a student-focused housing network for Ingolstadt, supporting more trustworthy connections across KU and THI.

Compatible

The platform considers lifestyle preferences and daily living habits, so matches are based on fit, not only room availability.

Flexible

RoomConnect supports different student situations: arriving in Ingolstadt for the first time, replacing a roommate, or looking for a better housing match.

Our vision: We want to build a safer and more transparent student housing network for Ingolstadt — one where roommate and housing decisions are based on trust, compatibility, and mutual choice.
