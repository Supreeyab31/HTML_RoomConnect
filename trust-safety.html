Trust & Safety

This page explains how RoomConnect tries to reduce scams and unsafe contact.

Verification & Identity Protection

RoomConnect verifies student identity using:

  • University email authentication
  • Optional student ID upload
  • Account protection alerts

Room Listing Verification

Listings are checked for:

  • Fake or reused photos
  • Unrealistic prices
  • Non-student landlords

Verified listings receive a green badge.

Secure Messaging

Messages are monitored for:

  • Scam keywords
  • Phishing attempts
  • Harassment or abusive language

Scam & Fraud Prevention

RoomConnect uses automated tools to detect:

  • Advance-fee scams
  • Fake payment requests
  • Suspicious account behavior

Privacy & Data Protection (GDPR)

Your data is protected under GDPR. RoomConnect:

  • Does not sell personal data
  • Encrypts sensitive information
  • Allows full data deletion on request
